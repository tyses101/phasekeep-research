Corrections made inside the checkpoints

ID or topicAffectsFirst made inOriginal statementCorrection
Wording on "correct results per run"V0.4 section 7V0.5 section 3The Buddy never gives more correct results per run than the same encoding without it.True only for the rejection-based Pauli model of that page, where the Buddy only removes outputs. It does not apply to error-correcting architectures.
Z-type faults and "commute through everything"V0.5 replyV0.6 section 3Z-type errors commute through everything.A Z-type fault commutes with ZZ, so the ZZ check cannot see it, but it does not commute with every computational operation: through a logical X rotation it becomes a mix of Z and Y.
Pauli-frame limit of the V0.5 chain modelV0.5V0.6 section 3(Not stated.)The chain model treats a fault as a Pauli frame that stays a Pauli: exact for idle and Clifford segments only.
CNOT Hadamard "incompatible with parity"earlier discussion (before V0.6)V0.6 section 3The CNOT implementation is incompatible with parity.Relative to a fixed ZZ check its midpoint is amplitude-dependent; relative to the transported check it is a valid code. It is compatible, restricted to a moved check.
E1V0.7 section 7V0.8 section 1An X fault on axis a "before the Hadamard" is invisible, fidelity 0.29.True only after the first CNOT. At position 0 the fault is detected with probability 1. A Pauli error leaves a Haar-random logical input with mean fidelity exactly 1/3; 0.29 and 0.32 were 100-sample estimates.
E2V0.7V0.8 section 1Controlled-phase routes 4 versus 1; edge geometry preserves local checks under inter-photon routes.Computed in V0.8: all four physical controlled-phase pairs preserve both local checks in both geometries; 4 (diagonal, diagonal) and 1 (edge, edge) implement the logical controlled phase. The V0.7 table was right; its tag was stronger than its computation.
E3V0.7V0.8 section 1Mean fidelity 0.826 for a wrong-angle controlled phase.The 300-sample estimate is replaced by the exact value 1 − 0.3(1 − cos d); at φ = 2.0 it is 0.8248.
E4V0.8 page and an earlier chat statementV0.9 section 1During conversion the verification "must be suspended or follow the transported group" (and, in chat, that no valid check exists).The first half is too strong. The transported checks exist at every step and read +1 exactly on converted logical states (worst deviation 8.9e-16). What stops being valid is the complete original check set. Whether Buddy can physically measure the intermediate weight-3 checks is open.
V0.9 ranking is for uniform Pauli noise onlyV0.9 section 4V1.0 section 1(Implicit) flagged is best.Its qubits were generic and noise uniform; under X-bias the ranking reverses. No numerical error was found in V0.9.
V0.9 "unusable = rejection" metricV0.9 section 7V1.0 section 1Selection by rejection and silent probability per round started.Not meaningful when rounds can fail to complete. V1.0 separates completion, rejection, heralding and silence.
Literature attribution, Majoranachat statement before V1.0V1.0 section 1The 2025 X/Z measurement asymmetry was attributed to a newer device.It belongs to the earlier 2025 device. All Majorana statements remain UNVERIFIED.

Mistakes made during construction (fixed before delivery, kept for the record)

  • V0.9: an inverted dominance test listed the worst configuration as optimal; a raw-weight versus stabilizer-coset-weight misreport; an over-stated concurrency assertion replaced by a distribution check; "5 times more second-order damage" corrected to about 4 times.
  • V1.0: a normal approximation that returned one run for a 41% click probability, replaced by an exact binomial threshold test that reproduces V0.9's 119,829; a silent-damage metric multiplied by completion probability that favoured joint rounds only because they fail more often; a shadowed variable; a float threshold too tight; "970 times" corrected to about 420 times; two page cells.
