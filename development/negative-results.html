Negative results

Status: working development record, 2026-11-27. Kept prominent on purpose. A negative result is a calculation that went against a proposal, or a limit of what a calculation can show. They are listed with the checkpoint that found them. All are inside the stated models.

#ResultFound inEvidence
1Moving between product and entangled bases always needs an entangling gate in the ideal model; conversion has a price.V0, V0.1DERIVED
2For one logical qubit, adaptive switching of geometry cannot save operations against the fused single-axis baseline.V0.2DERIVED
3The diagonal encoding is not uniformly more robust; X and H have no alternative single-axis route in it. Equivalent routes are not independent routes.V0.3DERIVED
4A Buddy parity check misses phase errors and can lower reliability: a harmful ancilla fault between the two CNOTs becomes an undetected logical error and a PASS.V0.4DERIVED, parameters ASSUMED

7Silence from a detector is not PASS once loss exists.V0.5DERIVED
8A PASS confirms the checked property only: a controlled phase with the wrong angle passes every local check.V0.7DERIVED
9The joint checks do not close the relative-phase gap either.V0.8DERIVED
10The joint code is not a superset of the local code's coverage; joint extraction creates correlated errors with the same number of silent logical cases.V0.8DERIVED
11Adaptive switching rejects slightly more than staying joint in every profile, and its benefit disappears at a conversion-fault probability near 2e-4 in the base profile.V0.8[DERIVED on ASSUMED workloads]
12Flagging does not remove silent damage, only moves the leading term from p to *p*²; under M3 it loses to the conventional joint round above p ≈ 1.1e-3.V0.9[DERIVED on ASSUMED fault model]
13The cooperative two-cat-qubit round tested is not fault tolerant, even with a verified cat.V0.9DERIVED
14Extra verification machinery increases the number of fault cases that leave some damage on the data.V0.9DERIVED
15With destructive readout the local geometry still needs inter-photon gates (4 per round); its advantage is a factor of 2.V1.0[DERIVED on ASSUMED rule]
16If a failed inter-photon gate aborts the round, completion falls to sn (for example 0.0039 for the joint round at s = 0.5); if failure disturbs the data, joint verification of unknown data is not usable below s ≈ 0.75.V1.0[DERIVED on ASSUMED s]
17Unheralded ancilla loss raises silent damage about 10 times (local) and about 60 times (flagged). Unheralded data loss sets a floor about 400 times the loss-free value for the flagged round.V1.0DERIVED
18The V0.9 ranking reverses under X-biased noise; the best configuration depends on the noise model.V1.0[DERIVED on ASSUMED bias]
19Repeated verification slows damage accumulation but does not stop it, and it costs runs.V1.0DERIVED
20Under some physical profiles the feasible set of verification configurations is empty.V1.0[DERIVED on a toy profile]

What these results do not mean

They do not show that PhaseKeep cannot work, and they do not show that it can. They show where the modelled proposal is fragile. Several rest on assumptions (destructive readout, abort versus benign failure) that the project has not yet tested. A model-dependent ranking is not a general statement about architectures.
