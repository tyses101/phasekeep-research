Status: working development record, written 2026-11-27. Provisional and model-based. Not part of Revision H. The Learning Library, Edition 03, Revision H (release edition-03-rev-h) is the frozen educational baseline, and nothing on these pages changes it. No experimental hardware measurement has been produced by this project, and no advantage over any architecture has been shown.

Evidence classes used on every page: DERIVED, ASSUMED, PROPOSED, UNVERIFIED, MEASURED (none exist), BLOCKED. They are the same classes as the frozen library, and Research status explains how they apply here.

Research status at a glance

This is where the work stands. Read each line for what it does not say.

LevelStateWhat it means
Mathematical derivationEstablished within stated models DERIVEDExact results for ideal gates, exact Pauli algebra and exact enumeration. They hold if the model holds, and say nothing about whether the model describes a device.
Computational validationAuthor-side only DERIVED11 checkpoint programs ran from clean extracted copies and printed ALL PASS. That is numerical consistency inside the models, not independent reproduction.
Proposed architectureOpen PROPOSEDBuddy configurations, Integrated Quantum Assurance (IQA), PALADIN, Docked Scheduling and shared ancillary hosting are proposals. Buddy's physical form is deliberately not fixed.
Physical feasibilityUnresolved, and the central question ASSUMEDThe V1.0 models show that probabilistic photon-photon interactions and photon loss may decide whether verification is usable at all. Whether suitable gates and readout exist is not known.
Hardware measurementNone MEASUREDNothing has been built or measured.

Guiding philosophy

We do not know everything, and perhaps we do not yet know everything about the things we believe we understand. Even established knowledge can contain unexplored relationships, overlooked possibilities, and opportunities for discovery.

PhaseKeep exists to explore those possibilities with curiosity, intellectual humility, scientific rigor, and a willingness to be proven wrong.

We question not to dismiss established science, but to discover what more it may have to offer.

The question

The central question and the subordinate question are kept word for word on one page.

How to read this section

  1. Research status explains the five levels above and the evidence classes.
  2. Timeline lists V0 to V1.0 in order. Each checkpoint page gives the question, model, assumptions, results, negative results, corrections, limits and reproduction command.
  3. Physical feasibility is the V1.0 summary and the most important page for a reader judging viability.
  4. Errata lists every correction with its date. Original checkpoint files are never edited; corrections live in separate dated documents.
  5. Negative results and Open problems are kept prominent on purpose.
