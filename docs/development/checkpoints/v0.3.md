# Checkpoint V0.3: leakage bias and operational redundancy

**Status: working development record, 2026-11-27. Index page for an original checkpoint.** Original files: [README](../../../checkpoints/v0.3/README.md), [page](../../../checkpoints/v0.3/docs/tetragon-map-v0.3.md), [handoff](../../../checkpoints/v0.3/CODEX_HANDOFF.md), [code](../../../checkpoints/v0.3/code/tetragon_map.py).

## Question

How badly can undetected leakage bias a verification estimate, and how many physically distinct routes implement the same logical gate?

## Model and assumptions

Ideal pure states, eleven parts. Illustrative availability and transmission numbers (0.9 per interaction; 98%, 94%, 96%, 99%) are [ASSUMED] and come from a review, not a measurement.

## Results

- Leakage bias is bounded: the XX and YX checks commute with ZZ, so a leaked fraction ε changes the measured value by at most about ε. Arbitrary errors outside the subspace are not covered [DERIVED].
- Redundancy exists for phase-type gates: Z and S can be carried by 1 axis (edge) or 2 (diagonal); X has 1 (edge) and none by a single axis (diagonal needs both axes); H has 1 (edge) and needs an entangling gate (diagonal) [DERIVED].
- For a logical controlled phase between two photons, the number of equivalent physical axis pairs is 1 (edge, edge), 2 (edge, diagonal) and 4 (diagonal, diagonal) [DERIVED].

## Negative or sobering results

The diagonal encoding is not uniformly more robust: for X and H it depends on more hardware, not less. Equivalent routes are not independent routes; shared sources or calibration would cut the benefit. One photon-to-photon entangling interaction is still needed, and that is the expensive kind.

## Corrections

The count "controlled-phase routes 4 versus 1" was carried forward in later pages and computed directly in [V0.8](v0.8.md) (erratum E2), which found the numbers right and the evidence tag stronger than the earlier computation had earned. See [Errata](../errata.md).

## Limits

Stabilizer bases and Clifford operations only; one photon-to-photon interaction type (controlled phase). Nothing independently reproduced.
