# Checkpoint V0: Tetragon transformation map

**Status: working development record, 2026-11-27. Index page for an original checkpoint.** Original files: [README](../../../checkpoints/v0/README.md), [page](../../../checkpoints/v0/docs/tetragon-map-v0.md), [handoff](../../../checkpoints/v0/CODEX_HANDOFF.md), [code](../../../checkpoints/v0/code/tetragon_map.py).

## Question

On one four-level photon (polarization H/V by OAM −1/+1, the Tetragon), which operations move between maximally entangled states and between measurement bases, and what does a single axis control?

## Model and assumptions

Ideal, noise-free operations and ideal pure states. Stabilizer bases and Clifford operations only. No loss, no fidelity, no devices. [ASSUMED] gate set: single-axis Cliffords, local Cliffords on both axes, one controlled phase.

## Results [DERIVED]

- Of the 24 permutations of the four Bell-type states, single-axis Cliffords produce 4 and local Cliffords on both axes produce all 24.
- For the one state tested, a rotation on polarization equals the transposed rotation on OAM. This is a property of that state, not of the operations.
- A classical time schedule adds no entanglement. A time bin in superposition used as a quantum control does (1 ebit in the example).
- Of the 15 Pauli-stabilizer bases in four dimensions, 9 are product bases and 6 are maximally entangled; they form 6 complete mutually unbiased sets of 5.
- Of 225 ordered basis pairs, 81 need a single-axis operation, 36 local operations on both axes, and 108 an entangling gate plus local operations.

## Negative or sobering results

Moving between a product basis and an entangled basis always needs an entangling gate in this ideal model, so conversion has a price. The "reversal of intuition" in the supplied review is mild: the entangled family is reachable from one axis because every maximally entangled two-qubit state is a one-sided unitary applied to a fixed state.
