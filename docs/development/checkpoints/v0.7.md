## Question

What exactly is verified (observable, expected value, measured value, cost), how complicated does a transported check become, and do local checks survive inter-photon operations on 2, 4 and 8 photons?

## Model and assumptions

Exact Pauli algebra and 16-dimensional state vectors. Gate ensembles, rates and productivity parameters are [ASSUMED]. Term count, support size and interaction order are structural proxies, not measurement costs.

## Results [DERIVED]

- The expected value of a check includes its sign as it is transported; a register that stored only "+1" would be wrong mid-route.
- Clifford steps keep the transported check a single Pauli string. Rotations about Paulis that commute with ZZ never grow it. A generic rotation about an anticommuting Pauli turns ZZ into two terms.
- All four physical controlled-phase routes between two Tetragons commute with both local checks.
- Three policies (measure, transport or defer the check) are compared around an excursion out of the code, and productivity is computed under two output-identifiability assumptions (see the page for the tables).

## Negative results

**A PASS confirms the checked property only.** A controlled phase with the wrong angle (φ = 2.0 instead of π) passes both local checks. Every Z fault is invisible to the parity checks, but invisible is not harmless.
