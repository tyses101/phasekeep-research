# Checkpoint V1.0: physical feasibility of verification

**Status: working development record, 2026-11-27. Index page for an original checkpoint. V1.0 names a research checkpoint, not a product or production maturity.** Original files: [README](../../../checkpoints/v1.0/README.md), [page](../../../checkpoints/v1.0/docs/buddy-verification-v1.0.md), [handoff](../../../checkpoints/v1.0/CODEX_HANDOFF.md), [code](../../../checkpoints/v1.0/code/buddy_v10_checks.py). The summary of what this checkpoint implies is on [Physical feasibility](../physical-feasibility.md).

:::plain In plain words
Earlier checkpoints assumed the checking machinery works. V1.0 asks what happens if photons can be lost and if the gates that connect two photons only sometimes succeed. In the modelled cases those two facts, not the cleverness of the check, decide whether verification is usable at all.
:::

## Question

Can the Buddy's verification rounds be physically carried out, once photon loss, probabilistic inter-photon gates, biased noise and readout asymmetry are included, and how does that change the V0.9 ranking of configurations?

## Model and assumptions (all [ASSUMED])

Ideal circuits with assumed Pauli, erasure and loss-like faults. Not measured hardware. Destructive photon detection, so an ancilla is a separate photon; K ancilla qubits per ancilla photon; loss per photon per two-qubit gate (ancilla loss heralded or unheralded, data loss latent or unheralded); inter-photon gate success probability s with a failed gate either aborting the round or being benign and retried; Z or X bias weight *b*; asymmetric readout; repeated rounds computed exactly; coherent amplification with exact binomial threshold tests; capability report v2 with Pareto selection under four physical profiles. Loss-like randomization is a modelling device and not a validated model of photon disappearance.

## Results [DERIVED on ASSUMED inputs]

- **Inter-photon gates per round (destructive readout, K = 1):** local 4, joint conventional 8, flagged 12, verified cat 18. With two ancilla axes per photon (K = 2) flagged falls to 8; with five-axis hosting verified cat falls to 8 and cooperative from 14 to 8. A nondestructive readout would lower the counts to 0 and 4, but its feasibility is unverified.
- **Completion probability $s^n$** if any failed gate aborts the round, at s = 0.75: 0.316, 0.100, 0.032, 0.0056 (local, joint, flagged, verified cat); at s = 0.5: 0.0625, 3.9e-3, 2.4e-4, 3.8e-6. Expected gate attempts per completed round at s = 0.5: 30, 510, 8,190, 524,286 if failures abort, but 8, 16, 24, 36 if failures are benign and retried.
- **Heralded ancilla loss** rejects 3.9% (local), 7.5% (joint), 14% (flagged) and 22% (verified cat) of rounds at 1% per photon per gate.
- **Unheralded ancilla loss** raises silent damage about 10 times (local) and about 60 times (flagged) at 1%.
- **Unheralded data loss** creates a floor: the flagged round's silent probability at 0.1% loss is 9.7e-4, about 400 times its loss-free value (2.3e-6).
- **Bias reverses the V0.9 ranking:** under strong X-bias (b = 100, noise at 1e-3 per location) the local round and a ZZZZ-only round beat flagged and conventional joint. Under Z-bias the joint codes are favoured, and at b = 100 flagged and conventional joint are nearly tied (1.44e-3 versus 1.48e-3). With Z-biased data only, conventional joint beats flagged. With biased ancillas only, flagged wins.
- **Repeated rounds** slow damage accumulation but do not stop it: at p = 1e-3 over 20 rounds, 0.20% of still-accepted flagged-joint runs are damaged (3.7% local, 7.7% with no verification), but only 64% of flagged runs are still accepted; at p = 1e-2 only 1.2% remain.
- **Coherent amplification:** a constant error of 0.01 rad needs 119,829 single-gate reference runs, 48 runs with 50 gates, or 2 runs of 256 gates (512 gate uses, a factor of 234). At 1% stochastic noise per gate it is still about 2,600 times fewer gate uses. An error that alternates in sign never accumulates. The principle is established; this is an instance, not an invention.
- **Shared mechanism:** verification, computation and characterization differ only in which Pauli product is measured. Whether one physical coupler can serve all three is not shown, and the merge of Buddy and Controlled Transformation is not claimed.
- **Capability report v2:** new fields (ancilla heralding, inter-photon gate success and failure type, photons consumed, per-observable readout error, nondestructive-readout availability). Under physical profiles the feasible set can be empty, and an empty set is a valid result.

## Negative results

The gap between abort and benign-retry is the whole feasibility question: if a failed gate disturbs a data photon, joint verification of unknown data is not usable at s below about 0.75. The local geometry's advantage is a factor of 2 in inter-photon gates, not a different kind of operation. Heralding the ancilla is worth more than the flag. Under X-bias the V0.9 ranking reverses. Repeated verification costs runs. See [Negative results](../negative-results.md).

## Limits

No gate-level model of a real heralded gate, no per-time loss, no conversion circuit under the physical model (it has two inter-photon gates, so completion s² applies directly), uniform or simple bias only. Literature at abstract or excerpt level, all UNVERIFIED. Nothing independently reproduced. The capability profiles are illustrations.
