# Checkpoint V0.8: local versus joint verification geometries

**Status: working development record, 2026-11-27. Index page for an original checkpoint.** Original files: [README](../../../checkpoints/v0.8/README.md), [page](../../../checkpoints/v0.8/docs/buddy-verification-v0.8.md), [handoff](../../../checkpoints/v0.8/CODEX_HANDOFF.md), [code](../../../checkpoints/v0.8/code/buddy_v08_checks.py).

## Question

Can the system change which errors it can see, by reorganizing the same four axes, without destroying the logical information, and does the change pay?

## Model and assumptions

Two Tetragons (four axes). Local geometry: checks ZZII and IIZZ. Joint geometry: the [[4,2,2]] code with checks ZZZZ and XXXX. Ideal elementary gates from an [ASSUMED] set; nothing says a photonic platform provides them. Independent Pauli faults with [ASSUMED] rates; ideal checks in the workload model. Gate counts, weights and photon spans are structural proxies.

## Results [DERIVED]

- Unknown logical information can be moved between the two geometries by a unitary: 4 elementary gates (two single-axis Hadamards and two inter-photon CNOTs), with 2 inter-photon gates in every shortest circuit. Measurement alone cannot do it.
- Conversion changes coverage of **future** faults only. A fault made before conversion keeps its detectability.
- The joint code is not a superset of the local code's coverage: 8 of 66 weight-1 or 2 coupling generators are seen only by the local code and 8 only by the joint code.
- No single elementary gate preserves the joint code. The shortest physical operations are pairs of controlled-phase gates, each pair a logical controlled phase.
- Coherent Z rotations on one axis are detected with probability sin²(ε/2) by the joint code.
- Adaptive switching pays on some workloads: 6.6% fewer accepted-wrong results than staying joint in the base profile, lost at a conversion-fault probability near 2e-4; 62.5% fewer in a strongly X-dominated profile, with a break-even above 1e-2 [DERIVED on ASSUMED workloads].

## Negative results

**The joint checks do not close the relative-phase gap:** a wrong angle on an in-code rotation passes both joint checks exactly as it passes both local checks. Joint extraction creates correlated errors (12 multi-axis data errors from single ancilla faults versus 4) with the same number of silent logical cases (4 versus 4). Adaptive switching rejects slightly more than staying joint in every profile. Four expectations were wrong; they are listed in section 8 of the page.

## Limits

Extraction is studied without flags or verified ancillae; the conversion-fault model is one example; the literature on [[4,2,2]] and stabilizer propagation is UNVERIFIED. The conversion circuit has two inter-photon gates, which becomes decisive under probabilistic gates ([V1.0](v1.0.md)).
