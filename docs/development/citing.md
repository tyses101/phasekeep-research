Cite the exact repository state you used. The stable, citable edition is the one named by the repository's `CITATION.cff`: *PhaseKeep Learning Library, Edition 03*, Revision H, by Tyler O'Bryan (release `edition-03-rev-h`). That metadata is unchanged by the development layer.

Accessed `<date>`.

Please say which revision or checkpoint you used. Checkpoint identifiers V0 to V1.0 are stable; later changes will appear as errata, not as edits to the original files.

## What not to cite as

- Do not cite a modelled number as a measured one. Every probability on these pages is a calculation under assumed rates.
- Do not describe the work as a built or demonstrated quantum computer, or as showing a physical advantage.
- Do not cite "V1.0" as a product version. It names a research checkpoint.
- Do not imply institutional affiliation or independent scientific endorsement. None exists.
