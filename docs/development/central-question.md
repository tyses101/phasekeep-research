# The central question

**Status: working development record, 2026-11-27.** The wording below is kept exactly as set by the project owner and used in every checkpoint from V0.8 onward.

:::key Central question
Can deliberate geometric organization and controlled transformation of photonic entanglement improve the efficiency, controllability, reliability or continuity of equivalent quantum operations?
:::

:::key Subordinate (programme) question
Can a photonic processor adapt its information geometry, verification requirements and physical execution routes in response to changing operational conditions, while achieving a measurable advantage over conventional architectures?
:::

## What would count as an answer

A positive answer needs a measured advantage on a physical system, against a stated conventional baseline, in one stated metric. Nothing in this project meets that bar. The checkpoints narrow what a viable system would need, and they have produced several [negative results](negative-results.md) along the way.
