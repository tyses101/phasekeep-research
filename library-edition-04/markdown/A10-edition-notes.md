## What this edition is

Edition 04 continues the PhaseKeep learning library from [Volume 15](15-relationships.md). It is a **working draft** and a **dated research snapshot as of 28 November 2026**. It describes the research up to that date; later findings go into a later edition or revision, recorded in a revision log like the one below, never as silent edits.

## Vocabulary

Edition 03 froze its core vocabulary and set a rule: a new term needs a literature check and a demonstrated purpose before it is added ([Appendix 6](../library/A6-edition-notes.html)). Edition 04 follows that rule:
- **Physics first:** each volume explains the physics before naming it.
- **New names stay provisional:** terms introduced after Edition 03 (for example QDE) are **working development terms**, marked as such in [Appendix 7](A7-glossary-additions.md).
- **Frozen meanings are untouched:** PK-M remains the four-mode atomic-vapor memory proposal, and PK-E the prepared-instruction execution scheme.
