Glossary Additions

Terms used in Edition 04, with their status

Working draft

This is Edition 04, a research snapshot as of 28 November 2026. It is not frozen and may be revised. Edition 03 Revision H is the frozen baseline.

Terms used in Edition 04 that are not in Edition 03's frozen glossary (Appendix 1), or whose use here needs a note. Working development terms are provisional: they are not part of the frozen vocabulary. The development site's register of terms is the authoritative status list for every PhaseKeep term.
