## Standard results, no single source

Superposition and phase, the no-cloning theorem, the 2/(d+1) measure-and-prepare benchmark, Malus's law, photon bunching at a beam splitter, and the counting formulas for hypercubes are standard textbook material. They are tagged [DERIVED] where we checked the arithmetic ourselves; nothing here depends on a particular textbook.

## PhaseKeep's own material

The scripts named in [Volume 9](09-research-lab.md), the A06 report, the component specification and the research website revisions are PhaseKeep's own working documents. They have been checked by running the code, and the R30 numbers were rerun by a second party, but none has had independent peer review.
