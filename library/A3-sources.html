Appendix 3

Sources and Read Status

What was actually read, what was supplied, what is unchecked

Honesty about reading: a source is only as good as how much of it was actually read. This page says what was done with each.

Specific sources

SourceUsed forRead status
Liu et al., heralded four-dimensional controlled-phase-flip gate with OAM, arXiv:2407.16356 (July 2024 preprint)Volumes 6, 7Read from the paper. Post-selection condition and the 1/4 and 1/8 factors confirmed. Not peer reviewed when read.
Hacker, Welte, Rempe, Ritter, A photon-photon quantum gate based on a single atom in an optical resonator, Nature 536 (2016), arXiv:1605.05261Volume 6Abstract only. Confirms fidelity 76.2 ± 3.6 % and a π phase. The 22 % transmission, 780 nm and 0.7 MHz figures are not in the abstract and remain unconfirmed.
Berkhout et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 105, 153601 (2010)Volume 9Abstract only. Eleven OAM states, author-reported check.
Mirhosseini et al., Nature Communications 4, 2781 (2013)Volume 9Abstract only. Separation efficiency above 92 %; transmission is not established by that figure.
Marrucci, Manzo, Paparo, Phys. Rev. Lett. 96, 163905 (2006), arXiv:0712.0099Volumes 4, 5Abstract only. Spin-to-orbital angular momentum conversion in inhomogeneous anisotropic media, linked to Pancharatnam-Berry phases.

Standard results, no single source

Superposition and phase, the no-cloning theorem, the 2/(d+1) measure-and-prepare benchmark, Malus's law, photon bunching at a beam splitter, and the counting formulas for hypercubes are standard textbook material. They are tagged DERIVED where we checked the arithmetic ourselves; nothing here depends on a particular textbook.

PhaseKeep's own material

The scripts named in Volume 9, the A06 report, the component specification and the research website revisions are PhaseKeep's own working documents. They have been checked by running the code, and the R30 numbers were rerun by a second party, but none has had independent peer review.
