Edition 03, Revision H · Draft for technical review

Open, independent research exploring Geometric Encoding Axies (GEA), Integrated Quantum Assurance (IQA), photonic information processing, and emerging quantum architectures through theoretical analysis, simulation, and collaborative investigation.

GEA and IQA are the two primary research pillars. IQA is an exploratory framework for verification, preservation, resource preparation, operational integrity and quantum information continuity. PhaseKeep pursues discovery without a predetermined technological destination. Naming decision and historical definition.

No experimental hardware measurements have been produced by this project.

Guiding philosophy

We do not know everything, and perhaps we do not yet know everything about the things we believe we understand. Even established knowledge can contain unexplored relationships, overlooked possibilities, and opportunities for discovery.

PhaseKeep exists to explore those possibilities with curiosity, intellectual humility, scientific rigor, and a willingness to be proven wrong.

We question not to dismiss established science, but to discover what more it may have to offer.
