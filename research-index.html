An open learning library and research record, from photonic encoding and quantum relationships to atomic registers, control and assurance.

Two pillars: Geometric Encoding Axies (GEA) and Integrated Quantum Assurance (IQA). No demonstrated quantum computer or hardware advantage is claimed. Evidence labels, failed approaches and corrections remain part of the story.

Historical versus current: Edition 03 is frozen. Edition 04, Volumes 15–23 and Appendices 7–10, is a dated research snapshot as of 28 November 2026. Working research records retain their own verification limits.
